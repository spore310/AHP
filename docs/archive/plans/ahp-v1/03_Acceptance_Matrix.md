### Packet review and commit procedure

For each packet, give two fresh reviewers the same Git tree, packet contract and acceptance criteria. Each reviewer reports independently before seeing the other report. Record their findings, points of agreement, dispositions and verification here. Resolve confirmed findings from either review, rerun affected checks, and record the local commit before advancing.
